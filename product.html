Product

One operating system for learning experiences.

Open LMX gives learning teams the building blocks to manage programs from curriculum design to learner success.

01

Program Builder

Create learning paths, modules, lessons, assignments, milestones, and outcomes.

02

Cohort Manager

Run batches, schedules, live sessions, facilitator assignments, and learner groups.

03

Learner Workspace

Give learners a focused dashboard for tasks, content, deadlines, submissions, and feedback.

04

Assessment Engine

Support quizzes, rubrics, projects, reviews, reflections, and skills-based evaluation.

05

Mentor Console

Help mentors review submissions, track risk signals, and provide timely feedback.

06

Analytics Layer

Measure completion, engagement, sentiment, learning outcomes, and program impact.

Designed for flexible learning models.

Use Open LMX for schools, internal academies, bootcamps, creator-led cohorts, partner enablement, and community education.
